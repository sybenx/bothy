bothy

A nostr relay of your own, in one click.

It runs on Cloudflare's free tier, in your own account. There is nothing to rent and nothing to keep running.

A bothy is a shelter in the Scottish highlands that someone built and left unlocked for whoever needs it. Cloudflare's free tier is a bit like that, and this is a relay that runs in it.

Deploy to Cloudflare Read the docs

Needs a free Cloudflare account and a free GitHub account. No card.

What you don't need

Running your own relay usually means standing up a machine and looking after it. This is the same relay without any of that underneath it.

  • a VPS
  • a domain name
  • Docker
  • a terminal
  • TLS certificates
  • a box at home
  • a monthly bill

Four steps

  1. Click Deploy to Cloudflare and pick your GitHub account. Cloudflare puts a copy of the code there and builds it.

  2. Wait about thirty seconds, then refresh the page — the dashboard doesn't update on its own. A Visit button appears; that's your relay.

  3. Open it and paste your npub into the claim form. The first pubkey to claim a relay owns it, permanently.

  4. Copy the wss:// URL into your nostr client's relay list. Nothing in the Cloudflare dashboard needs configuring.

What it does

Fetches your history, once, by itself

Claiming starts a backfill: bothy reads the relay list you have already published, finds the relays you post to, and pulls your past notes in from them a page at a time until it has caught up. You don't have to do anything, and it stops when it's done.

Takes writes from you and the people you follow

It reads the follow list your client already keeps, so there is no second allowlist to maintain, and strangers are turned away. Everyone but you is size-capped, rate-limited, and cut off well before your storage fills.

Receives your mail

Gift-wrapped DMs from anyone, readable only by you. A query that would match one gets an auth challenge; a query that wouldn't is answered as though your inbox were empty — so nobody can count your mail by watching what gets refused.

Hosts one private group

One group, one admin, you. Invite links that expire and can't be reused twice. Everything in it is kept out of every unauthenticated read, including the member list. The chat is deleted when the conversation ends, because a conversation is not an archive.

Wakes your phone

Optional web push that says which room and what kind of thing happened, and nothing else. Not the message, not the sender. Off unless you set a key.

Answers to your key, not a dashboard

Ban an event, block an address, rename the relay — every management command is a request you sign from the command line. The status page stays read-only, which is what makes it safe to leave public.

What it costs

  • 5 GBstorage
  • 100,000database writes per day
  • $0per month

That's Cloudflare's free tier, and your relay shows both limits as bars on its own status page, so there is never a bill-shaped surprise. Writes from anyone but you stop at half the storage, which keeps the rest of it for your own archive.

What it is not

No zaps, no media uploads, no NIP-05 hosting, no multi-region scaling, no moderation queue, and no public write mode. These are decisions rather than gaps, and the reasoning for each one is written down.

bothy is built to be one of the two-to-four relays your relay list already names, not your only one. Pair it with a permissive public relay and you get both: your own filtered archive of the people you actually follow, and a general-purpose inbox that already does the spam filtering you would otherwise have to build.
